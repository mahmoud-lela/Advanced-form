Coverage Information

Occupational Accident Insurance

Provides medical expense coverage and disability benefits for work-related injuries that occur while operating your commercial vehicle. This coverage helps protect you when you're injured on the job, covering medical bills, lost wages, and rehabilitation costs.

  • Medical expenses up to policy limits
  • Temporary total disability benefits
  • Permanent partial disability coverage
  • Accidental death and dismemberment benefits
Physical Damage Coverage

Protects your commercial vehicle against physical damage from collisions, comprehensive losses, and other covered perils. This includes damage to your truck, trailer, and attached equipment.

  • Collision coverage for accidents
  • Comprehensive coverage for theft, vandalism, weather
  • Coverage for attached equipment and tools
  • Towing and labor costs
Non-Trucking Liability (Bobtail)

Provides liability protection when you're operating your commercial vehicle for non-business purposes or when not under dispatch. This coverage fills the gap when your motor carrier's insurance doesn't apply.

  • Bodily injury liability coverage
  • Property damage liability coverage
  • Protection during personal use of truck
  • Coverage between loads or off-duty
Workers Compensation

Required by law in most states, this coverage provides benefits to employees who are injured or become ill due to work-related activities. It covers medical expenses and wage replacement for covered injuries.

  • Medical expense coverage for work injuries
  • Wage replacement benefits
  • Vocational rehabilitation services
  • Death benefits for beneficiaries
Occupational Compensation

An alternative to traditional workers compensation for independent contractors and owner-operators. Provides similar benefits but designed specifically for the trucking industry's unique needs.

  • Medical benefits for occupational injuries
  • Disability income protection
  • Flexible coverage options for contractors
  • Industry-specific benefit structures
